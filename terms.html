The rules for using Heightcast, written the same way the rest of the site is: plainly, and without pretending to know more than we do.

  1. 01
    It's an estimate, not a measurement.

    Heightcast runs three published methods and shows you where they agree. Even at its best, the Khamis-Roche method lands within about ±5 cm (±2 in) roughly nine times out of ten — which is why we show a range instead of one confident-looking number. Real people land outside their range.

  2. 02
    It is not medical advice.

    This page is a calculator, not a doctor, and it has never seen you. If you're worried about your growth — too fast, too slow, or just different from your friends — talk to a doctor or to a parent or guardian. A real examination (a doctor can even X-ray a hand to read bone age) beats any website.

  3. 03
    Nothing on the internet will make you taller.

    Once your growth plates close, no supplement, stretch, powder or programme adds permanent height. We don't sell any, we don't link to any, and we never will. If a site promises you inches, it's selling something.

  4. 04
    No guarantee, and no fee.

    Heightcast is free and provided as-is. We can't promise it is free of mistakes, that it will always be online, or that your adult height will fall inside the range it shows. Use it out of curiosity — not to make a decision that matters.

  5. 05
    Using it means you accept that.

    By using this page you agree it's an informal estimate, and that whoever made it isn't responsible for what you decide off the back of it. That's the entire agreement — there's nothing to sign, and nothing to cancel.

  6. 06
    The card you download is yours.

    The shareable picture is drawn on your device and belongs to you. Share it or don't. Remember it shows an estimate, so if you post it, post it as one.
