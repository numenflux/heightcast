We collect
nothing at all.

That's the whole policy. Here it is in longer words, in case you want to check the working.

  1. 01
    There is nothing to collect, because we never ask.

    No name, no email address, no account, no password. There is no sign-up, no waitlist and no contact form on this site — a form would collect something, so there isn't one.

  2. 02
    The numbers you type never leave your device.

    Your age, height, weight and your parents' heights are used by your own browser to do the maths, exactly like a calculator app works with the wifi switched off. They are never uploaded, and the moment you close the tab they're gone.

  3. 03
    No cookies, no analytics, no trackers, no ads.

    Nothing counts your visit, follows you to other sites or builds a profile of you. No outside company's code runs on this page at all: the font, the icons and the maths are all part of the page itself.

  4. 04
    Nothing is stored on a server, because there's no database.

    Heightcast is a handful of plain files. There is nowhere for information about you to be kept, which also means there's nothing that could leak, be sold, or be handed over.

  5. 05
    The shareable card is made on your device too.

    The picture is drawn in your browser and saved straight to your device. We never see it, and we couldn't if we wanted to. What you do with it afterwards is entirely up to you.

  6. 06
    The one thing we can't switch off: the web host.

    To show you any page, a web host has to send it to you — so it briefly sees that a browser asked for a file, along with your IP address, in its ordinary logs. That happens on every website you have ever visited, we don't read those logs, and they never contain anything you typed here.

  7. 07
    If you're a young person — or a parent reading over a shoulder.

    Because nothing is ever collected, there is no data to ask us for, correct, or delete, and nothing to give permission for. You can use this page without telling it anything about who you are.

  8. 08
    If this ever changes, this page changes first.

    If Heightcast ever adds something that collects information, it will be written here, in plain words, before it goes live.
